NEUROLOGY CLINIC LETTER — EPILEPSY REVIEW

Dear [Patient Name],

Thank you for attending your epilepsy review clinic on [DATE].

SEIZURE HISTORY:
You have a known diagnosis of focal epilepsy with secondary generalisation. Your last confirmed seizure was approximately 8 months ago, which is encouraging progress.

CURRENT MEDICATIONS:
- Levetiracetam 1000 mg twice daily
- Lamotrigine 150 mg twice daily

PLAN:
1. We will continue current medication doses as seizure control has improved significantly over the past year.
2. Blood tests requested: Full blood count, Liver function tests, Renal function.

DRIVING REGULATIONS (IMPORTANT):
Under current regulations, you must not drive if you have had a seizure in the past 12 months. You are approaching the 12-month seizure-free mark. Please contact the DVLA/relevant authority before resuming driving. Do NOT drive until formally cleared by both your neurologist and the licensing authority.

LIFESTYLE ADVICE:
- Avoid alcohol as it can lower the seizure threshold.
- Maintain a regular sleep schedule — sleep deprivation is a known seizure trigger.
- Take medications at the same time each day. Do NOT miss doses.
- Avoid unsupervised swimming or bathing.
- Inform your employer of your condition if operating machinery or at heights.

WARNINGS:
- If you experience a seizure lasting more than 5 minutes, or three or more seizures without regaining consciousness between them (status epilepticus), call emergency services (999/112) immediately.
- Do NOT stop anti-epileptic medications suddenly — this can precipitate prolonged seizures.

FOLLOW-UP:
Neurology review in 12 months or sooner if seizure recurrence.
