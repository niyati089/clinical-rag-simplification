Diagnosis: Invasive ductal carcinoma, right breast, Grade 2, pT1cN0M0 (Stage IA)
Receptor Status: ER+, PR+, HER2-negative
Multidisciplinary Team (MDT) Decision Date: [DATE]

TREATMENT PLAN:
Following MDT discussion, the recommended treatment plan is:

1. SURGERY (Completed):
   Right wide local excision and sentinel lymph node biopsy — completed [DATE]. Margins clear. Sentinel node negative.

2. RADIOTHERAPY:
   Adjuvant whole-breast radiotherapy to the right breast. 
   Schedule: 15 fractions over 3 weeks.
   To commence within 4–6 weeks of surgery.

3. ADJUVANT ENDOCRINE THERAPY:
   Tamoxifen 20 mg once daily — to be taken for 5–10 years.
   Important: Do not stop Tamoxifen without discussing with your oncologist.

SIDE EFFECTS OF TAMOXIFEN TO REPORT:
- Unusual vaginal bleeding
- Sudden shortness of breath or leg swelling (risk of blood clots)
- Vision changes
- Severe mood changes

SIDE EFFECTS OF RADIOTHERAPY (EXPECTED):
- Skin redness and soreness in the treated area (usually temporary)
- Fatigue (usually improves after treatment ends)

IMPORTANT:
- Inform any treating doctor that you are on Tamoxifen before any procedures.
- Avoid pregnancy during treatment — Tamoxifen is harmful to a developing baby.
- Use reliable contraception.

FOLLOW-UP:
Oncology review 4 weeks after radiotherapy completion.
Annual mammogram of the right breast.
Bone density scan (DEXA) if entering menopause while on Tamoxifen.
