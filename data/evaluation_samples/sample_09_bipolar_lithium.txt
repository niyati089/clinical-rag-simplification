IMPORTANT INSTRUCTIONS FOR LITHIUM:
- Take Lithium at the same time every night.
- Do NOT miss doses or take a double dose if missed.
- STAY WELL HYDRATED — dehydration (from illness, heat, exercise) raises lithium levels to toxic ranges.
- Reduce lithium if you have diarrhoea or vomiting and contact your psychiatry team.
- AVOID NSAIDs (ibuprofen, naproxen) — they raise lithium levels dangerously.
- Low-salt diets can also raise lithium levels — do not make drastic dietary changes without medical advice.
- Inform any new prescriber that you are taking Lithium.

SIGNS OF LITHIUM TOXICITY — SEEK EMERGENCY CARE IMMEDIATELY:
- Coarse hand tremor, confusion, slurred speech, unsteady walking, nausea and vomiting together — these indicate lithium toxicity, which is a medical emergency.

RELAPSE PREVENTION:
- Maintain regular sleep patterns — sleep disruption can trigger manic episodes.
- Limit alcohol.
- Contact your care coordinator if you notice early warning signs: decreased sleep need, increased energy, racing thoughts.
