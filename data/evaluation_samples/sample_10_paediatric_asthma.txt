DIAGNOSIS:
Mild persistent asthma, currently partially controlled.

CURRENT INHALER REGIMEN:
- Salbutamol (Ventolin) 100 mcg inhaler — 2 puffs as needed for symptoms (reliever)
- Beclometasone dipropionate 100 mcg inhaler — 1 puff twice daily every day (preventer)

USAGE REVIEW:
Reliever (Salbutamol) used on more than 3 days per week over the past month — this indicates asthma is not fully controlled and step-up treatment may be needed.

INSTRUCTIONS FOR INHALERS:
1. The PREVENTER inhaler (brown/orange — Beclometasone) must be used every day, even when your child feels well. It reduces inflammation over time and prevents attacks.
2. The RELIEVER inhaler (blue — Salbutamol) should be used only when symptoms occur. If your child is using it more than 3 times per week, contact the asthma nurse.
3. Always use the spacer device with both inhalers for children under 12.
4. Rinse mouth after preventer inhaler to prevent oral thrush.

ASTHMA ACTION PLAN:
- GREEN: No symptoms — continue preventer daily.
- YELLOW: Mild wheeze or cough — use reliever (2 puffs), if improvement in 20 minutes resume normal activity.
- RED: No improvement after reliever, breathing fast, lips/fingernails going blue — CALL 999 IMMEDIATELY. Give reliever every 20 minutes while waiting for ambulance.

TRIGGERS TO AVOID:
Pet dander, cigarette smoke, cold air, viral infections.
Ensure school has an up-to-date asthma action plan and a spare reliever inhaler.

FOLLOW-UP:
Paediatric asthma nurse review in 6 weeks.
Spirometry arranged for the next appointment.
