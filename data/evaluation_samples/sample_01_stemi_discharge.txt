MEDICATIONS AT DISCHARGE:
1. Aspirin 75 mg once daily — continue indefinitely
2. Ticagrelor 90 mg twice daily — continue for 12 months; do NOT stop without consulting your cardiologist
3. Atorvastatin 80 mg once daily at night
4. Metoprolol succinate 50 mg once daily
5. Ramipril 2.5 mg once daily — to be titrated by GP
6. Pantoprazole 40 mg once daily

IMPORTANT INSTRUCTIONS:
- Do NOT miss any dose of Ticagrelor. Stopping this medication early significantly increases the risk of stent thrombosis (a life-threatening blockage of the stent).
- Avoid non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) such as ibuprofen.
- No heavy lifting for 4 weeks.
- No driving for at least 4 weeks or as advised by your doctor.
- Report any chest pain, shortness of breath, or palpitations immediately to the emergency department.
