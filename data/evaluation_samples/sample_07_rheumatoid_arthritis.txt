METHOTREXATE MONITORING:
Methotrexate can affect the liver and blood counts. Blood tests (FBC, LFT, U&E) must be performed every 8 weeks. Do NOT skip blood tests. If your GP contacts you about abnormal results, stop Methotrexate and contact the rheumatology helpline immediately.

IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION FOR METHOTREXATE:
- Take Methotrexate ONCE A WEEK only. Taking it daily is life-threatening.
- Take Folic acid every week to reduce side effects.
- Do NOT take trimethoprim or co-trimoxazole antibiotics — they interact dangerously with Methotrexate.
- Avoid alcohol entirely or limit to very small amounts.
- Use reliable contraception — Methotrexate causes serious birth defects. Discuss with your doctor before planning a pregnancy.
- Carry your Methotrexate alert card at all times.

WARNING SIGNS — SEEK URGENT ATTENTION:
- Mouth ulcers, severe sore throat, fever, unusual bruising, or persistent cough — these may indicate serious blood or lung side effects.
