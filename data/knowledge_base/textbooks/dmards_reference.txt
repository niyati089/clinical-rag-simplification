DISEASE-MODIFYING ANTIRHEUMATIC DRUGS (DMARDs) — REFERENCE GUIDE

CONVENTIONAL SYNTHETIC DMARDs (csDMARDs)

METHOTREXATE (MTX)
- Class: Folate antagonist; antiproliferative and anti-inflammatory
- Indications: Rheumatoid arthritis (first-line DMARD), psoriasis, psoriatic arthritis
- Dosing: Weekly administration — NEVER daily. Start 7.5–15 mg/week; titrate up to 25 mg/week
- Co-prescribe: Folic acid 5 mg once weekly (take the day after Methotrexate) to reduce toxicity
- CRITICAL SAFETY: Methotrexate taken daily instead of weekly has caused fatal toxicity — patients must be explicitly counselled
- Monitoring: FBC, LFT, U&E every 8 weeks (when stable)
- Serious adverse effects:
  * Hepatotoxicity (liver damage) — monitor LFTs; avoid alcohol
  * Myelosuppression (bone marrow suppression) — monitor FBC; seek urgent care for fever, sore throat, bruising
  * Pulmonary toxicity (methotrexate pneumonitis) — report persistent dry cough, dyspnoea
  * Teratogenicity — absolutely contraindicated in pregnancy; reliable contraception required during treatment and for 3 months after stopping in both sexes
- Drug interactions:
  * Trimethoprim / Co-trimoxazole — AVOID (enhances myelosuppression risk)
  * NSAIDs — use with caution (can increase MTX levels)
  * Probenecid — increases MTX toxicity
- Patients should carry a Methotrexate alert card

HYDROXYCHLOROQUINE
- Mechanism: Inhibits toll-like receptor signalling; anti-inflammatory
- Indication: Mild RA, systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE)
- Dose: 200–400 mg daily (not exceeding 5 mg/kg ideal body weight/day)
- Monitoring: Annual ophthalmology review — risk of retinal toxicity with long-term use (>5 years)
- Generally well tolerated; GI side effects common initially

SULFASALAZINE
- Indication: RA, ankylosing spondylitis, inflammatory bowel disease with arthritis
- Dose: 500 mg/day, increasing to 2–3 g/day over weeks
- Monitoring: FBC and LFTs initially, then every 6 months
- Avoid: Sulfonamide allergy, G6PD deficiency

BIOLOGIC DMARDs

ANTI-TNF AGENTS (e.g., Adalimumab, Etanercept, Infliximab)
- Indication: Moderate-to-severe RA failing csDMARDs
- Key safety considerations:
  * Increased risk of serious infections (bacterial, tuberculosis reactivation, fungal)
  * Screen for latent TB before initiating (Mantoux test / IGRA)
  * Avoid live vaccines during treatment
  * Monitor for signs of infection; suspend if serious infection develops
  * Contraindicated in active TB, severe heart failure (NYHA III/IV), demyelinating disease

JAK INHIBITORS (e.g., Baricitinib, Tofacitinib)
- Oral targeted synthetic DMARDs
- Monitor for: venous thromboembolism, cardiovascular events, lipid changes, haematological changes
