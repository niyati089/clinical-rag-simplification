ANTICOAGULATION THERAPY — CLINICAL GUIDELINES SUMMARY

INDICATIONS FOR ANTICOAGULATION
Anticoagulation therapy is indicated for:
- Venous thromboembolism (VTE): deep vein thrombosis (DVT) and pulmonary embolism (PE)
- Non-valvular atrial fibrillation (AF) to reduce stroke risk
- Mechanical heart valve replacement
- Acute coronary syndrome (as adjunct to antiplatelet therapy)
- Prevention of VTE in high-risk surgical patients

DIRECT ORAL ANTICOAGULANTS (DOACs)
DOACs are preferred over warfarin for most indications due to predictable pharmacokinetics and reduced monitoring burden.
Common DOACs: Apixaban, Rivaroxaban, Dabigatran, Edoxaban.

DURATION OF TREATMENT
- Provoked VTE (reversible risk factor): minimum 3 months
- Unprovoked VTE: minimum 3–6 months; consider long-term after risk-benefit assessment
- Atrial fibrillation: usually indefinite in moderate-to-high-risk patients (CHA2DS2-VASc ≥ 2 in men, ≥ 3 in women)

MAJOR BLEEDING RISK
Anticoagulants increase the risk of bleeding. Patients should report:
- Unusual bruising
- Prolonged bleeding from cuts
- Blood in urine (red or dark brown)
- Black or tarry stools
- Unexpected heavy menstrual bleeding
- Coughing or vomiting blood
- Severe or unusual headaches

DRUG INTERACTIONS
NSAIDs increase bleeding risk significantly and should be avoided in patients on anticoagulants.
Aspirin co-administration increases bleeding risk and should only be used when specifically indicated (e.g., post-ACS).

RENAL FUNCTION MONITORING
Most DOACs are renally cleared. Renal function (eGFR) should be monitored at least annually and more frequently if eGFR < 60 mL/min/1.73m².

REVERSAL AGENTS
- Warfarin: Vitamin K, Prothrombin Complex Concentrate (PCC) for urgent reversal
- Dabigatran: Idarucizumab (Praxbind)
- Factor Xa inhibitors: Andexanet alfa

PATIENT COUNSELLING KEY POINTS
- Take medication at the same time every day
- Never double a missed dose
- Inform all healthcare providers (including dentists) of anticoagulant use
- Carry an anticoagulant alert card at all times
- Avoid contact sports or activities with high injury risk
